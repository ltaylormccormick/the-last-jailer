## Full description

> Three years ago, Kaelen Veyr was a knight of the King's Guard. Now he's a jailer — the last
> one — bound to a black iron door beneath a dead tree, keeping watch over something that isn't
> supposed to still exist.
>
> **THE LAST JAILER** is a choice-driven dark fantasy RPG where every decision leaves a mark.
> Nothing is cosmetic. The words you choose, the mercy you show or withhold, the alliances you
> build or burn — all of it shapes who Kaelen becomes, what the prisoner reveals to him, and how
> his story ends.
>
> **A STORY THAT REMEMBERS**
> Branching dialogue and consequential choices run through the entire campaign — flags you set
> in an early chapter can resurface a dozen chapters later. Nothing is filler. Every path is
> written to matter.
>
> **A COMPANION, NOT A MONSTER**
> The prisoner behind the door isn't what Kaelen — or you — expect. As trust builds (or doesn't),
> the truth of who's really been imprisoned, and why, unfolds one choice at a time.
>
> **REAL RPG SYSTEMS**
> - Turn-based combat against a roster of escalating threats
> - Courage and Honour stats that gate entire choices and endings
> - An inventory of items and keepsakes carried across the whole campaign
> - Trophies for the paths and secrets you find
> - Multiple save slots, so you can replay pivotal choices differently
>
> **A FULL CAMPAIGN**
> The first three chapters are completely free — play the entire opening arc, no strings
> attached. If you're hooked, a single one-time purchase unlocks the rest of Kaelen's story to
> its end.
>
> No ads. No subscriptions. No timers. Just a story worth finishing.
>
> *The Last Jailer is written for players who want their choices to cost something. Choose
> carefully.*

# Privacy Policy — The Last Jailer

**Effective date:** August 31, 2026

This policy describes what data The Last Jailer ("the app") collects, why, and how it's handled.
It was written to describe exactly what the app's code actually does — nothing more, nothing
inferred or aspirational.

## Summary

The Last Jailer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no account
system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network code of its own. It
stores your game progress locally on your device, and uses Google Play's own billing system to
process the one optional in-app purchase.

## What the app stores, and where

The app saves two small pieces of data locally on your device, using Android's standard
SharedPreferences storage. Neither ever leaves your device except as part of your own device's
Android backup (see "Backups," below).

1. **Game save data** — your chapter and scene progress, character stats (courage, honour,
   health, gold, level, experience), inventory items, unlocked trophies, and story choice flags,
   for each of your save slots. This is purely fictional gameplay data. It contains no personal
   information, and there is no way for us to connect it to you as an individual — we never
   receive a copy of it at all.
2. **Purchase status** — a single true/false flag recording whether you've unlocked the full
   story. This flag is set locally on your device only after Google Play confirms a completed
   purchase (see below). We do not store your payment details, transaction history, receipts, or
   any other purchase data ourselves.

## Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase ("Unlock Full Story"), handled entirely by Google
Play's billing system. When you make a purchase, Google — not the app's developer — collects and
processes whatever information it needs to complete the transaction (such as your payment method
and Google account details), under [Google's own Privacy
Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Play Store Terms of
Service](https://play.google.com/intl/en-us/about/play-terms/). The app only ever receives
confirmation that a purchase succeeded — never your payment information itself.

## Backups

The app allows Android's standard Auto Backup feature, which may include your local save data in
the private backup Android already keeps for your device under your own Google account. This is
a feature of your device's operating system, not something the app transmits to any server the
developer operates or controls, and the developer has no access to it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not use analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting tools of any kind.
- It does not show ads or use any advertising SDK.
- It does not require or support account creation, sign-in, or sign-up.
- It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other
  personal information.
- It does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any to share.
- It does not use any network connection of its own; the only network activity that occurs is
  Google Play's billing system communicating with Google's servers to process a purchase.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from
anyone, regardless of age, because it does not collect personal information from any user.

## Data deletion

Because all game data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us, uninstalling
the app permanently deletes it. There is no account or server-side record to separately request
the deletion of.
